# SIM swap indicators

## Before the swap

- A support contact asking to move the phone number, especially from an IP the customer has never used.
- The request arrives without a recent login from the customer's own devices.

## During and after

- The phone number changes, then a password reset by SMS follows within hours.
- The reset is completed from an unrecognised device or network.
- A new-device login follows the reset, then a new payee and a large transfer.

## Response

- Hold the phone change for analyst review when support contact came from an unfamiliar network.
- After any phone change, do not rely on SMS step-up for 72 hours: hold instead (step-up policy §2).
- Genuine customers who lose their phones usually contact support from their home network, and their next login is from home.
